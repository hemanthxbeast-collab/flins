# JARVIS phase 5: conversational voice

Goal: talking to FLINS feels like a conversation, not a series of button presses, and it
hears you correctly. Architecture row: "conversational voice, barge-in, better wake word;
risk: false wakes". Also the speech-recognition upgrade parked during phase 3.

## What is built
1. **Speech recognition on the GPU.** `accuracy: max` (Whisper large-v3-turbo) with
   `stt_device: auto` or `cuda`. Measured on this laptop (RTX 4060): 0.3 s per sentence
   against 2.0 s for the old CPU model, about 2.1 GB of video memory, all five test sentences
   exact. A large model is never run on the CPU: `max` without a GPU uses `small`. One
   throwaway run at start-up so the first sentence isn't the slow one.
2. **Follow-up listening.** After FLINS answers with a question (or is waiting for your yes /
   no), it listens again by itself: short low beep, no hotkey. Silence, or "thanks" / "that's
   all", ends the conversation quietly. At most 6 follow-ups per hotkey press.
   `voice.follow_up`: `question` (default) | `always` | `off`; `voice.follow_up_s`.
3. **Barge-in.** Press the hotkey while FLINS is talking: it stops within about half a second
   and listens. Works for answers and for spoken alerts. The Windows voice is now driven
   through SAPI directly so it can be cut off mid-sentence; Piper stops through sounddevice.
4. **Wake word, fewer false wakes.** The small model still spots "FLINS" on every burst of
   speech (on the CPU). The main model must then hear "FLINS" in the same audio before FLINS
   wakes, and its words are the command. `voice.wake_confirm` (default true).

## Files
- `voice/loop.py`: `_conversation`, `_listen`, `interrupt`, follow-up rules.
- `voice/tts.py`: `TTS.stop()`; `SapiTTS` over SAPI COM with a pyttsx3 fallback; `PiperTTS.stop`.
- `voice/audio.py`: `record(..., start_timeout_s=)`.
- `voice/wake.py`: `WakeWord(confirm=)`.
- `voice/stt.py`: `GPU_ONLY_MODELS`, `warm_up`. `voice/factory.py`: model choice, wiring.
- `assistant/core.py`: `Assistant.awaiting(source)`.
- `settings.py`, `config/flins.example.yaml`: `follow_up`, `follow_up_s`, `wake_confirm`.
- `tests/unit/test_j5_voice.py` (24 tests; no microphone, speakers or model).

## Safety notes
- The kill switch is unchanged: "stop" by voice still pauses within a second, and barge-in
  makes it reachable while FLINS is talking.
- Follow-up listening opens the microphone without a key press, so it is bounded: default
  only after a question, a few seconds to start speaking, at most 6 in a row, and nothing is
  recorded to disk. Anything that sends a message or changes a mode is still read back and
  needs your yes.
- No voice barge-in (stopping FLINS by speaking over it): without echo cancellation FLINS
  would hear itself. The microphone stays ignored while it talks.
