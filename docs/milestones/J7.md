# JARVIS phase 7: proactive (briefings, deadline nudges, end-of-day summary)

Until now FLINS only speaks when spoken to, or when a reminder you set comes due. This phase
lets it speak first, a little. Architecture row: "briefings, deadline nudges, end-of-day
summary, interrupt policy; risk: spam; hard caps".

**Off by default** (`assistant.proactive.enabled: false`). Everything it produces goes to you
only: a note in your self-chat and, when allowed, a spoken line. Nothing here can message a
contact.

## What is built
1. **Morning briefing** at a time you set: what is due today, what is overdue, how many other
   open tasks, drafts waiting for approval, and (if the weather tool is on with your usual
   place) one weather line.
2. **Evening summary** at a time you set: done today, still open from today, tomorrow, and
   message counts for the day.
3. **Deadline nudge**: a heads-up some minutes before a task is due ("In 60 minutes: lab
   report"). Not for tasks created at short notice, and once per task and due time.
4. **On demand**: "brief me", "how does my day look", "evening summary", and
   `flins briefing [morning|evening]`.

All text is built by code from your own tasks and from counts. No AI model is involved, and no
message text from contacts is included (only numbers).

## Interrupt policy (code, tested)
| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Paused ("stop") | nothing is delivered |
| Focus or away (and your own modes on them) | written to your self-chat, not spoken |
| Quiet hours (default 23:00 to 07:00) | written, not spoken |
| More than `max_per_day` (default 6) proactive messages today | dropped, and audited |
| Several nudges due at once | one combined message |
| A briefing with nothing to say | skipped |
| FLINS wasn't running at the briefing time | sent when it starts, if within 3 hours; else skipped |

On-demand briefings ignore the cap and the quiet hours: you asked.

## Rules
1. At most once: a briefing or nudge is logged before it is written, so a failure can't
   repeat it every second.
2. The cap is absolute for anything FLINS starts by itself.
3. Speaking needs: voice on, `speak: true`, mode normal or working, outside quiet hours.
4. Audit rows (`assistant.proactive`): kind, outcome, counts. No titles, no text.
5. Reminders you set are untouched: they are yours, not FLINS's initiative, and don't count
   toward the cap.
