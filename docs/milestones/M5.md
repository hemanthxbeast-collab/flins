# M5: Automatic holding replies, FLINS in your self-chat, session summary

## Goal
While a busy mode is on, FLINS **sends the fixed holding reply by itself** to the contacts the
policy allows (inner and trusted, for small talk / availability / "where are you"), talks back to
you **inside your WhatsApp self-chat** (command answers, approval prompts, urgent alerts, the
summary), and ends each session with a **per-message summary**.

AI-written free-form replies still need your approval (they unlock per contact in M9).

## What gets sent automatically
Only `AUTO_SAFE` decisions: one of your templates from `policy.yaml`, written in the third person,
already through the output guard. Before it goes out, the outbox checks again:
- FLINS isn't paused, a busy mode is still on and hasn't ended
- the person wrote within 24 h, and **you haven't replied to them yourself** since
- at most **one holding reply per chat per session**, at most `per_contact_max_auto_per_hour`
  per contact, and the global hourly/daily caps
- dry run is off

It waits a random `send_delay_seconds` (default 20–90 s) first, so it doesn't look instant.
Leaving the mode (`I'm done`, expiry) or `stop` cancels anything still queued.
`policy.yaml` → `auto_replies: holding_only` (default) or `off`.

## FLINS in your self-chat
Every note starts with "🤖", and FLINS ignores self-chat messages that start with it or that it
sent itself, so it can never react to its own notes.
- answers to commands (`status`, `working mood…`, `y3`, …)
- approval prompts: `#3 Kavya: "are you free?" → draft: "…" · y3 / n3 / e3 text`
- urgent alerts: `⚠ URGENT from Rahul: "call me now"`
- expiry warnings and the end-of-session summary
Self-chat notes skip the contact checks (it's you) and are still sent while paused (so "Paused."
reaches you). `policy.yaml` → `self_chat_notes: true` (default).

## Summary
On `I'm done` or expiry: counts, then one line per message: who, what they said (short), and
what FLINS did (sent holding reply / waiting for you / told you / ignored), with urgent items first.

yourself; once per session; per-contact cap; `auto_replies: off`; dry run. Self-chat: answers are
queued as notes, never trigger commands again, still sent while paused. Approval prompts and urgent
